Where We Help You Settle

Luxe Settle currently assists students moving to Schengen countries and the UK.

Italy

  • Bologna, Milan, Rome, Florence, Turin, Padova, Venice

Trusted guidance for university towns and cultural capitals.

United Kingdom

  • London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds

Ideal for diverse programs with great public transport links.

Germany

  • Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Cologne

Popular for engineering, design, and business degrees.

France

  • Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Toulouse, Lille

Historic campuses and vibrant student life.

Spain

  • Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Seville

Sun, culture, and buzzing international communities.

Netherlands

  • Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Eindhoven, Utrecht

High quality of life and English-taught programs.

Scandinavia

  • Sweden (Stockholm), Norway (Oslo), Denmark (Copenhagen), Finland (Helsinki)

Safe cities with excellent public services.

Others in Schengen

  • Belgium, Austria, Portugal, Switzerland

We can advise on additional cities—tell us your plans.

If your destination is not listed, contact us — we may still be able to help.
