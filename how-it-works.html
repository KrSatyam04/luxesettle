How Luxe Settle Supports Your Journey

A clear, human-guided process from your first message to your first weeks in your new city.

Step 1 – Share Your Requirements

Fill out a simple form with your destination, dates, budget, accommodation type, and extra services needed.

Step 2 – Consultation & Clarification

We may follow up via WhatsApp or email to clarify your needs and expectations.

Step 3 – Curation & Shortlisting

Our team researches, verifies, and curates realistic options from trusted sources, landlords, and agencies.

Step 4 – Review & Selection

You review the options, ask questions, and choose the one that feels right.

Step 5 – Confirmation & Booking Support

We guide you through the booking process and help ensure key details are correct.

Step 6 – Arrival & Settle Services

We coordinate your airport pickup, first transfer, and essential appointments where applicable.

Step 7 – Aftercare Support

For your first weeks, we remain available for basic questions about your city, transport, and essentials.
