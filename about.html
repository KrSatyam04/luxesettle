About Luxe Settle

Built by people who once moved abroad as students — now helping the next generation arrive with confidence.

The Problem

Students and parents struggle with safe, transparent accommodation and arrival in Europe/UK. Information is scattered, online listings are risky, and local processes are confusing.

The Solution

Luxe Settle is a high-touch, human-powered concierge. We curate real accommodation options, coordinate your arrival, and guide you through essentials so you can focus on your studies.

Our Mission

To make international relocation feel safe, smooth, and dignified for students and their families — no matter where they come from.
