Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from students and parents.

Do you own or manage the properties you suggest?

+

No. We don’t own properties. We curate and suggest options from third-party landlords, agencies, and platforms, and help you navigate them.

Can you guarantee a visa or residence permit?

+

No. We do not influence visa or residence permit outcomes. We provide accommodation concierge and basic guidance only.

Do you work with all universities?

+

Yes. We support students headed to any accredited university across our covered destinations.

How long does it take to receive accommodation options?

+

Typically within a few days after we confirm your requirements. Timing can vary by season and city.

Can you help groups of students or friends?

+

Yes. Tell us how many people you’re moving with, and we’ll tailor the search and arrival support.

How do I pay for your services?

+

We share secure payment options once we scope your needs. Fees may vary by country and complexity.

Do you work with parents directly?

+

Absolutely. We’re happy to coordinate with parents or guardians to ensure everyone is informed.

What if I don’t like any of the suggested options?

+

Tell us what isn’t working and we’ll refine the search or suggest alternative services.
