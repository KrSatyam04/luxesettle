Our Services

Tailored support for every stage of your move abroad.

A

Accommodation Concierge

We personally search, shortlist, and verify accommodation options for you — from rooms in shared apartments to private studios and student residences. No random online listings. Only curated options.

  • Personalized search based on your budget & preferences
  • Communication with landlords or agencies
  • Basic contract guidance (not legal advice)
  • Coordination of viewing (virtual or in-person if possible)
T

Arrival & Transfers

From the airport to your new home, we coordinate your arrival so you never feel stranded.

  • Airport pickup & taxi booking
  • Transfer coordination to your accommodation
  • First-day local tips and support
E

Essentials & Documents

We guide you through local essentials so you can start your life smoothly.

  • Assistance with bank account appointment (if applicable)
  • Guidance for tax ID / Codice Fiscale / equivalent
  • Health insurance guidance (where applicable)
  • Appointment support for residence permits or related steps (explanatory only)
L

Luxe Complete Settle Package

Our highest-touch service. From finding your home to handling your arrival and helping with essential setup, this package gives you peace of mind at every step.

  • Accommodation concierge included
  • Airport pickup and day-one assistance
  • Essentials & document guidance
  • Priority support via WhatsApp/email
